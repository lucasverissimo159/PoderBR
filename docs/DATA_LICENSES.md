# Data Licenses and Attribution

## IBGE (Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística)
- **License:** Brazilian Open Data (Dados Abertos). Law No. 12.527/2011 (Access to Information Law).
- **Constraints:** Free for commercial and non-commercial use.
- **Attribution:** Required. Must cite "IBGE" and the specific survey (e.g., "Source: IBGE, Sistema Nacional de Índices de Preços ao Consumidor" or "Source: IBGE, Pesquisa Nacional por Amostra de Domicílios Contínua").

## Ipeadata (Instituto de Pesquisa Econômica Aplicada)
- **License:** Open Data.
- **Constraints:** Free for public use.
- **Attribution:** Required. Must cite "Ipeadata" and the primary source listed in the metadata (e.g., "Source: Ipeadata / Ministério da Economia").

## CEPEA / ESALQ / USP (Centro de Estudos Avançados em Economia Aplicada)
- **License:** Proprietary / Academic, usually permitted for journalistic and informational use.
- **Constraints:** Data scraping should be done carefully to avoid rate-limiting or server strain. Wholesale redistribution of raw databases is generally discouraged without permission, but calculating derivative indexes (like our Affordability Index) is standard practice.
- **Attribution:** Strictly Required. Must cite "CEPEA/ESALQ/USP". Example: "Nominal beef prices sourced from Indicador do Boi Gordo CEPEA/B3".
